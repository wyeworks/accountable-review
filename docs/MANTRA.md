# The Accountable Review Mantra

**Preserve the difficulty of engineering judgment. Eliminate the difficulty of understanding the explanation.**

**Desirable difficulty:** Understanding engineering tradeoffs, assessing correctness, challenging assumptions, and developing ownership of the change.

**Undesirable difficulty:** Decoding dense language, chasing cross-references, reconstructing scattered context, and figuring out why information matters.

**Our principle:** Accountable Review should make the code easier to understand, not the decisions easier to outsource.

The agent does the work of explaining. The human does the work of judging.
