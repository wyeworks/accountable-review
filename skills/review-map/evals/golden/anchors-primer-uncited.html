Understanding Ruby on Rails ActiveModel::Dirty
archived_at_changed? vs saved_change_to_archived_at?

Every attribute carries two predicates and they are true at different moments. archived_at_changed? compares memory against the loaded row and is true before the save; saved_change_to_archived_at? reports what the save just wrote and is only true after it. Which one a callback wants therefore depends on when the callback runs, and both mistakes fail silently.

The callback runs after the write, so it is the second form it has to ask.
